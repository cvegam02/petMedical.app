# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Veterinary professionals working in the clinic: lead vets, staff vets, assistants, and admin coordinators. Context: between consultations, under time pressure, looking up a patient's history before the owner walks in, or logging a record immediately after a procedure. They are experts at their job and expect the software to keep up — not train them.

## Product Purpose

petMedical.app is a multitenant SaaS for veterinary practice management. It lets individual veterinarians and veterinary hospitals manage owners, pets, clinical records, and appointments from one place. Success looks like: a vet can pull up a pet's complete clinical history in under five seconds and add a new consultation record without leaving the page they're on.

## Brand Personality

Precise, trustworthy, efficient. The software equivalent of a well-organized clinic: clean surfaces, everything in its place, nothing superfluous. Professional warmth — serious without being cold, because the work involves living beings, not sales metrics.

Reference feel: Stripe and Vercel — the reliable professional tool. Clean, unambiguous, confident. It respects your expertise by staying out of your way.

## Anti-references

- **SaaS cliché**: purple-blue gradients, hero metrics with big numbers, glassmorphism cards, "powerful synergies" copy
- **Consumer pet apps**: pawprint icons, childish illustrations, pastel color washes, playful rounded everything
- **Corporate dashboard**: navy and gold, stiff formal layouts, no visual warmth, suits-and-briefcases energy
- **Generic healthcare software**: hospital teal, heavy table grids, 2005-era UI density with no hierarchy

## Design Principles

1. **Data forward**: The clinical record is the product. The UI should be nearly invisible — a frame, not a distraction. Hierarchy serves the data, not the brand.
2. **Expert trust**: Assume the user knows what they're doing. No over-explanation, no confirmation dialogs for routine actions, no onboarding friction in the daily flow.
3. **Precision over decoration**: Every element earns its presence. If it doesn't communicate or enable action, it doesn't exist.
4. **Professional warmth**: Serious and clean, but not sterile. The work matters — the interface can acknowledge that through thoughtful typography, spacing, and occasional considered color.
5. **Speed in the clinic**: Every interaction should complete in the minimum possible steps. A vet between consultations has 30 seconds.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA minimum. Used in varying clinical lighting conditions (bright overhead lights, dim exam rooms). Color is never the sole carrier of meaning. Text sizes lean slightly larger than defaults for quick scanning under pressure.
